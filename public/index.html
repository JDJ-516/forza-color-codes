Suspension baseline

Damper coefficients are physical N·s/m at the damper, not Forza bump/rebound slider values. No game-specific conversion, anti-roll-bar, alignment, or differential recommendation is claimed.

How this is calculated

Tire diameter = rim × 25.4 + 2 × width × aspect / 100 (mm). Circumference = π × diameter. Speed (km/h) = RPM × circumference (m) × 60 / (gear ratio × final drive × 1000). RPM after shifting = shift RPM × next ratio / current ratio.

Wheel rate = (2π × frequency)² × sprung corner mass. Spring rate = wheel rate ÷ motion ratio². Damper coefficient = 2ζ√(wheel rate × sprung corner mass) ÷ motion ratio². This idealized model ignores tire stiffness, aero load, and nonlinear/progressive suspension.

Use the tire dimensions for the driven wheels. The target-speed result solves the same formula for final drive; it does not establish whether the engine can pull that gearing.
